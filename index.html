questions

Good to know.

How do I receive the card?

You get a private link (e.g. wishlab…/for-ayesha). Send it to them on WhatsApp, Instagram — anywhere. It opens in the browser, nothing to install.

How fast is delivery?

Standard delivery is within 24 hours of sharing your details. The Forever plan is prioritised same-day.

Can I see it before they do?

Yes — you approve a preview first. Small wording tweaks are free; the Forever plan includes two full revisions.

What occasions do you cover?

Birthdays, Eid, anniversaries, proposals, graduations, thank-yous — if it deserves a smile, we can build it.

How long does the link stay live?

At least one year, free. You can ask for it to stay up longer — most links stay forever.
