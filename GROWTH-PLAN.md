## 1. AUDIENCE ATTRACTION (TikTok-first playbook)

### Why TikTok
The reference account (dream devx) gets 34K+ likes per video with ONE format: screen-record
the card opening with emotional audio. The product IS the content — every card is a video.

### The 4 video formats (rotate daily)
1. **POV walkthrough** — "POV: tum ne apni behen ko ye bheja aur wo ro padi 🥹" — screen-record
   the full experience (lock → counter → photos → writing) with a trending emotional audio.
2. **Reaction video** — film the RECIPIENT's face while they open it (with permission).
   Reaction > product. This is the highest-converting format on TikTok.
3. **Making-of** — "Banate hue" screen-record: type the name, generate the link, send on
   WhatsApp. Shows how easy ordering is.
4. **Occasion countdown** — "Mother's Day mein 5 din reh gaye — abhi order karo" (urgency).

### Rules that make it work
- **CTA in every caption:** "Comment 'CARD' — link DM karenge" (comments boost reach; DMs convert).
- **Watermark demo cards:** small "wishlab.pages.dev" in the demo videos so shares advertise.
- **Post at 8-10pm PKT** (desi TikTok peak).
- **Urdu + English mixed captions** — feels personal, ranks in both.
- **Duet/stitch bait:** "Send this to someone who deserves it" endings.

### Beyond TikTok
- **WhatsApp Status marketing** — your own status + ask every buyer to share theirs (free virality
  inside family networks — the actual buying unit in Pakistan).
- **Instagram Reels mirror** — repost every TikTok.
- **Occasion calendar spikes** (prep content 2 weeks early):
  Valentine (Feb) · Mother's Day (May) · Father's Day (Jun) · Eid ×2 · Independence Day (Aug) ·
  birthdays (evergreen, 60%+ of orders).
- **Referral engine:** after delivery, message buyer: "Apne dost ko refer karein — dono ko
  Rs 500 off." Family chains = compounding orders.
- **Free lead magnet:** a name-only mini card (no lock/photos) anyone can generate — "Try free,
  upgrade to full magic." Captures WhatsApp numbers.
- **Fiverr/Etsy listing** for international buyers ($ pricing already on site).

### KPI targets (first 90 days)
- 60 TikToks posted → 3 should cross 50K views (volume finds the hit)
- DM → order conversion ≥ 20% (prefilled WhatsApp template helps)
- 40% of buyers from referrals by day 90
