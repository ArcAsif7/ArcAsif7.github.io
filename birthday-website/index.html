A personalized birthday website for someone you love

A birthday website turns your greeting into an experience: the recipient opens a link, sees their name and reads a message chosen by you. WishLab creates the page for you, so you do not need to design or code it.

What can go into the surprise?

The Spark plan includes a theme, their name, your message and the animated greeting. Enchant adds a passkey screen, a life counter, up to six photo memories and a typed letter. Forever supports up to twelve photos, custom colours and wording, and two revisions.

Choose a plan based on what you want to share. For a short greeting, a thoughtful message may be enough. For a partner, friend or family member, photos can tell a longer story.

How to prepare your birthday message

  • Start with the name or nickname they like.
  • Include one specific memory and what it means to you.
  • Choose photos you have permission to share.
  • Finish with a wish for their next year.

For example: “Happy birthday, Ayesha. I still smile when I remember our rainy-day picnic. Thank you for making ordinary days feel special. Here is to another year of good stories.” Replace these details with your own.

Ordering and sharing on WhatsApp

Send the occasion, their name and your message through the order link. Add photos if your chosen plan includes them. You receive a preview to approve before sharing the finished link. The standard delivery time advertised by WishLab is 24 hours after details are shared; confirm availability when you order.

Once your page is ready, paste its link into your WhatsApp message. The recipient opens it in a browser without installing an app. Check the preview on your own phone before sending the surprise.

Birthday website or digital gift card?

This is a personalized greeting website. It is not a prepaid shopping card or a voucher with a cash balance. Choose it when you want to send a personal message and memories.

Before you order

Check the current plan details, delivery timing and how long your page will stay available. Share only information and photos you are comfortable putting on the page; a memorable passkey is not a substitute for protecting sensitive information.
